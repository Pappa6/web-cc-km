Our Story

A name that means
compassion — woven into every piece we sell.

Tucked into Chandidas Market in Durgapur, Karunamayee has spent years building something simple: a place where every saree is chosen the way you'd choose one for your own family. From rich Kakinara silks and bridal weaves to everyday elegance, each piece is picked for its craft, its colour, and the story it tells.

Alongside our sarees, our menswear counter dresses the men of the house — Punjabi kurtas, dhoti sets and festive suiting for every celebration, big or small.
